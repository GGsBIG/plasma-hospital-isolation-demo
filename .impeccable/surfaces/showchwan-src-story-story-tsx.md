# Surface: 秀傳醫院 demo (showchwan)

Scope: the whole Show Chwan demo page. Visitor mode: Persuade (customer decides Plasma is the path). Audience: hospital management and IT, first in a projected sales meeting with a presenter, then alone from a sent link (desktop or phone). Job: understand today's four pains and the Plasma path that removes each. Content: four pains, five steps (four solve one pain each, the fifth is the head-office result), results comparison, head-office roster (demo numbers, labeled). Constraints: Brobridge brand (see PRODUCT.md), A–D branch names, no NCSIST wording.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One continuous scroll story. Narrative beats on the left, the isometric flow diagram pinned on the right and changing with each beat. Refuses the slide deck of tabs and equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Brobridge paper white, ink #111, logo red #b42622 as the only accent. Square corners, 2px ink rules between beats, 6px red header rule, bar buttons. Problems are red dashed flows and red square markers. Solutions are solid ink flows with moving dots. No green, no rounded cards, no glass.

STORY: First the visitor sees the head office cannot count its nurses. Then they see four pains, each marked on the diagram. Then Plasma's four steps, each clearing a named pain. They end on one roster and a comparison table.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The left 36% holds the heading 總院統計不出來 at about 54px, then the first pain beat with a big red numeral. The right 64% is the pinned diagram: four branches, manual consolidation and the report, with the red ① tag on the diagram. A thin vertical progress rail marks beats. The header holds chapter links and the autoplay bar button.
